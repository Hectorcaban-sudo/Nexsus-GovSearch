# Nexsus GovSearch UI Mockups

High-fidelity conceptual mockups of the key Lightning Experience pages. These illustrate the target user interface for the Pipeline Dashboard, Opportunity record with AI Brief, Contacts & Org Chart, and Research Console.

Images were generated as part of the design process. Add the corresponding PNG/JPG files to this folder (`docs/mockups/`) for version control.

## 1. Pipeline Dashboard

**File**: `pipeline-dashboard.jpg`

AI-optimized list of best-fit opportunities with relevancy scores, strategic fit, incumbent, type, and due dates. Top summary cards and right-side Pipeline Scout Agentforce panel.

Key elements:
- Top 5 Best-Fit Leads card
- Total Pipeline Value
- New High-Score Alerts
- Sortable opportunities table (sorted by AI Relevancy Score)
- Agentforce sidebar with recent recommendations and chat

## 2. Opportunity Record + AI Brief

**File**: `opportunity-ai-brief.jpg`

Opportunity detail page (example: Army IT Modernization Recompete) with custom AI enrichment fields, related notices/documents/contacts, prominent AI Brief section, and Opportunity Intelligence Agentforce panel on the right.

Key elements:
- Relevancy Score gauge
- Incumbent, Priority, NAICS tags
- AI Brief with Incumbent Strengths, Competitive Whitespace, Pursuit Prioritization
- Agentforce chat for follow-up questions

## 3. Contacts & Agency Org Chart

**File**: `contacts-org-chart.jpg`

Hierarchical agency/office navigation, senior decision-maker contact list with influence ratings and bio snippets, visual org chart, and Research & Relationship Agentforce panel suggesting call plans and key influencers.

## 4. Research Console

**File**: `research-console.jpg`

Unified search across Awards, Solicitations, and Vendors with filters (Agency, NAICS/PSC, Value, Expiration). Market spend charts, Competitor Intelligence, Partner Matchmaking, and Agentforce research assistant.

---

These mockups guide the Lightning page layouts, FlexCard/LWC designs, and Agentforce panel placement. They can be used as reference when building the actual Lightning record pages and app in the org.
